Role & Goal:
Act as a Senior UI/UX Designer. Create a clean, modern, high-conversion B2B industrial manufacturing website landing page for "ARK Polychem Private Limited" (amatic.in), based on an industrial fluid conveyance & hose catalog. ### Frame & Layout Settings:
- Device: Desktop 1440px width (Fluid Auto Layout, 12-column grid, 24px gutter, 80px side margins)
- Responsive Variants: Desktop (1440px), Tablet (768px), Mobile (375px)
- Auto Layout: Applied vertically with proper gap hierarchy (8px, 16px, 24px, 48px, 80px)

### Design System & Theme:
- Primary Color: Deep Industrial Navy Blue (#0F243E)
- Secondary Accent: Safety Orange / Energetic Amber (#E05315 or #D9381E)
- Backgrounds: Clean Pure White (#FFFFFF) with alternating Neutral Cool Gray (#F8FAFC)
- Text & Surfaces: Dark Slate (#1E293B) for body, Slate (#64748B) for secondary text, White borders (#E2E8F0)
- Typography: Inter or Poppins (Headings: Semi-bold/Bold, Body: Regular 16px, Line-height 1.5)
- UI Elements: Subtle rounded corners (8px for cards, 6px for buttons, pills for tags), soft box-shadows (0 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.06))
